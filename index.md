# Hatch & Learn

Learning is more fun when there's an egg about to hatch! Finish lessons to hatch your egg, then raise, dress up, and evolve your pet. There are 20 eggs to collect, and each one teaches a new subject.

Every egg and pet in the app was drawn by a 10-year-old.

## Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Please [open an issue](https://github.com/josephcantelmo/hatch-and-learn/issues).

**Common questions**

- **How do I hatch my egg?** Do 10 lessons. Each lesson is 5 questions.
- **How do I change the math difficulty?** On the first screen, tap **Adult**, then pick Easier, Just right, or Harder.
- **Where is the Birthday Egg?** Pick your birthday month in Settings. The Birthday Egg shows up that month.
- **How do I start over?** Tap **Adult**, then **Start over**. This erases your child's eggs, pets, and lessons.
- **Does the app need the internet?** No. Everything works offline and stays on your device.
