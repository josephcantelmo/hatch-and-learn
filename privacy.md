# Hatch & Learn Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 21, 2026*

Hatch & Learn is a learning app for kids. We built it to be private from the start.

## The short version

**Hatch & Learn does not collect, store, or share any personal information.** Nothing you do in the app leaves your device.

## What the app saves, and where

To remember your progress, the app saves these things **only on your device**:

- Your grade, lessons, and answers
- Your eggs, pets, coins, and items
- An optional nickname and birthday month (only the month, never the full date)
- Settings, like math difficulty and sounds

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. A grown-up can also erase it any time from the Adult screen with **Start over**.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts or sign-ups
- No ads
- No analytics or tracking
- No third-party services or software that collect data
- No internet connection is used
- No location, contacts, camera, or microphone access
- No chat or messaging with other people

## Sharing pet photos

The app can make a picture of your pet. If you tap **Share photo**, a grown-up must pass a quick check (a parental gate) first. After that, the phone's own share menu opens, and you choose where the picture goes, like Messages or your Photos. The app never sends anything by itself.

## Children's privacy

Hatch & Learn is made for children. Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children. This follows the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
